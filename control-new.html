Duck Bay Management Plan

ShellMAP Management Plan issued 23/12/2025.

Combined environmental closure criteria
  • Salinity below 32.5‰
  • 7-day Smithton rainfall ≥40 mm
  • 3-day Smithton rainfall ≥25 mm
  • Duck River at Scotchtown Rd ≥5.0 m³/s
Re-opening after combined environmental closure
  • All environmental criteria satisfied
  • Rainfall ceased, or daily rainfall is less than 10% of the 7-day criterion
  • 48 hours of satisfactory salinity above the criterion
  • Salinity may be demonstrated by 48 hours ShellPOINT data or three manual low-tide measurements at T0, T24 and T48

Important: Formal ShellMAP closure/open decisions, including biotoxin, phytoplankton, sewage, microbial and other regulatory events, can override these environmental indicators. Check the current ShellMAP Classification Notice before harvesting.
