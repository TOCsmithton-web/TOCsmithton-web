Pitt Water Management Plan

Current NRE Tasmania Pitt Water Management Plan — published October 2025.

Combined environmental temporary closure criteria
  • Zone 1: salinity <30‰ • 7-day rainfall ≥45 mm • Coal River flow ≥6 m³/s
  • Zone 2: salinity <27‰ • 7-day rainfall ≥40 mm • Coal River flow ≥6 m³/s
  • Zone 3: salinity <29‰ • 7-day rainfall ≥40 mm • Coal River flow ≥6 m³/s
Re-opening after environmental closure
  • All environmental conditions in the Management Plan must be satisfied.
  • 48 hours of satisfactory salinity at low tide must be demonstrated.
  • For rainfall closures, rainfall must have ceased or daily rainfall must be less than 10% of the applicable 7-day rainfall criterion.
  • Riverflow and rainfall criteria must remain satisfied through the 48-hour period.

Zone 2 additional requirement: shellfish microbial meat testing is fortnightly. Biotoxin testing is weekly for representative stock; water phytoplankton monitoring is monthly.

Important: Formal ShellMAP opening/closure decisions, including biotoxin, phytoplankton, sewage, microbial and other regulatory events, can override these environmental indicators. Check the current NRE Classification Notice before harvesting.
