How the prediction works

Smithton model: a cosine curve joins the published high/low tides. Wind effect scales linearly from ±0.4 m at 35 km/h, with intermediate compass directions blended and capped at the cardinal effect. Pressure correction is (1013.25 − forecast hPa) × 0.01 m; the baseline and immediate response are provisional. BOM explains the approximate pressure effect and response lag. The four-hour start matches the falling adjusted curve to the same cycle normalised to a 1 m low, four hours beforehand. This estimates access timing rather than a weather-driven shift in the astronomical low. Confirm this rule against actual lease experience.

Each daylight low tide is checked against wind and gusts across its entire proposed window. The window with calmer wind and more favourable pressure is selected each day. Higher pressure is favoured; low or rapidly falling pressure is flagged because water can remain on the lease. The provisional low-pressure warning band is below 1005 hPa; the higher-pressure band starts at 1020 hPa. These bands need checking against lease experience. Wind direction is where wind comes from. Smithton uses the farm’s N/E water-retention and S/W water-drainage examples; other locations do not assume favourable directions.

BOM provides astronomical tide times and heights for the selected reference port. Its heights use the BOM prediction datum; they are not lease depth, MSL or automatically equivalent to your chart datum. Actual lease tides can differ because of the bay and its entrances. The Smithton timing adjustment is +1 hour. Smithton’s separate blue curve adds the farm wind estimate and an approximate pressure correction.

Pressure and wind: BOM tide predictions exclude weather effects. Low pressure and winds holding water in the bay can make the lease tide higher than the table; higher pressure favours lower water levels. This page uses those conditions to rank and flag reference work windows, and keeps the original BOM heights unchanged.

BOM cautions that secondary-port predictions use limited observations and are less accurate. They are not suitable for navigation. Read the BOM tide explanation and cautionary note.

Forecasts beyond seven days are tentative. Missing heights, weather, or tide coverage are shown explicitly. AI commentary, when connected, explains the supplied forecast; the displayed windows remain calculated from the data.
