How the prediction works

Each daylight low tide is checked against wind and gusts across its entire proposed window. The window with calmer wind and more favourable pressure is selected each day. Higher pressure is favoured; low or rapidly falling pressure is flagged because water can remain on the lease. The provisional low-pressure warning band is below 1005 hPa; the higher-pressure band starts at 1020 hPa. These bands need checking against lease experience. Wind direction is displayed as the direction it comes from; no favourable direction is assumed until lease experience confirms it.

BOM provides astronomical tide times and heights for Pirates Bay, a nearby secondary prediction location. Its heights use the BOM prediction datum; they are not lease depth, MSL or automatically equivalent to your chart datum. Actual Boomer Bay tides can differ because of the bay and its entrances. No local time or height correction is assumed.

Pressure and wind: BOM tide predictions exclude weather effects. Low pressure and winds holding water in the bay can make the lease tide higher than the table; higher pressure favours lower water levels. This page uses those conditions to rank and flag reference work windows, and keeps the original BOM heights unchanged.

BOM cautions that secondary-port predictions use limited observations and are less accurate. They are not suitable for navigation. Read the BOM tide explanation and cautionary note.

Forecasts beyond seven days are tentative. Missing heights, weather, or tide coverage are shown explicitly. AI commentary, when connected, explains the supplied forecast; the displayed windows remain calculated from the data.
